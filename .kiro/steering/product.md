# RAKTABEEJ — Product Constraints

Source of truth: `docs/Plan.md`. Schedule: `docs/Milestones.md`.

Top-down open-world vampire survival RPG. Godot 4 (.NET), C#. Solo dev.
Ship target: Steam Early Access, Windows + Steam Deck.

## Design pillars — every feature must serve at least one

1. The anachronism is the game. A predator from 1861 in 2070 Kolkata.
2. Every meal is a permanent decision. Feeding costs memory, irreversibly.
3. Hauntings are mechanical, never cosmetic. They change what the player CAN DO.
4. The city is an antagonist with a body: Corpsec, UV grids, a real case file.
5. Power costs identity. Beast and Man are mutually corrosive.
6. Readable first, pretty second. UI renders at native resolution, always.

## Out of scope until explicitly scheduled

Vehicles (except tram-hopping), firearms, minigames beyond vending machine and
transit terminal, multiplayer, Android, iOS, consoles, web.

If a request appears to need something on this list, stop and say so.
